Job Description — Executive Chef

Protea Hotel Kampala · Kitchen Department
Executive Chef
Reports to: General Manager
Job purpose
To lead and manage all kitchen operations across the property, ensuring the consistent delivery of high-quality food products that meet Marriott brand standards, guest expectations, and the Group's financial targets. The Executive Chef is accountable for food cost control, hygiene compliance, team development, and kitchen performance against the Balanced Scorecard.
Key responsibilities
1. Oversee daily kitchen operations including preparation, presentation, and service across all food outlets.
2. Manage food cost within the approved budget and drive a 30% reduction in food waste year on year.
3. Ensure 100% pass rate on all kitchen hygiene and food safety audits.
4. Lead, mentor, and develop all kitchen staff; build succession depth in key positions.
5. Design, review, and update menus seasonally in line with guest feedback and revenue targets.
6. Maintain full compliance with Marriott brand standards for food quality and presentation.
7. Collaborate with the F&B Manager on covers growth, event catering, and upselling initiatives.
8. Submit weekly kitchen reports covering waste, staffing, training, and cost performance.
Required qualifications
Diploma or degree in Culinary Arts, Hotel Management, or a related field. A professional certification in food safety and hygiene (e.g. HACCP) is required.
Experience
Minimum 7 years of progressive kitchen experience, with at least 3 years in an Executive Chef or Sous Chef role in a 4-star or 5-star hotel environment. Experience in a Marriott or international hotel brand is an added advantage.
Key competencies
Leadership and team development · Food cost management and financial discipline · Creativity and menu innovation · Hygiene and food safety standards · Attention to detail · Communication and guest orientation · Resilience under pressure
Linked KPIs
Food waste reduction — 30% YoY Kitchen hygiene audit pass rate — 100% Upselling training completion — 100% of team
